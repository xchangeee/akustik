# Listening: EQ v1 vs v2, amp volume (2026-11-20)

EQ v1 = DSP preset slot 2 ([eq-v1-flat.md](eq-v1-flat.md)), EQ v2 (Harman) = slot 3 ([eq-v2-harman.md](eq-v2-harman.md)).
Before listening, v2 was checked in the configurator against the filter files (HPF BW 24 29.8 Hz on both outputs, all PEQ bands as in the files, unused bands 0 dB).
All as reported by the user; SPL is dB(C) at the listening position (iPhone).

## Amp volume (SMSL A300) per EQ

| EQ | Amp | SPL at seat | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| v1 | < 30 | | bass too thin |
| v1 | 40 | | works |

| v2 | 30 | 60–65 dB(C) | everyday / night |
| v2 | 35 | 65–70 dB(C) | everyday / night |

| v2 | > 45 | | probably too much, or only for short periods |

## How v2 sounds (vs v1)

- At moderate volume (amp 45): bass louder and more "subby", but not boomy/wobbly.
- At lower volumes: bass also a bit better.
- Outside the room: bass can be heard, depending on where you stand. Not much in the staircase.

## How v1 sounds at loud levels

- At loud levels (amp 50–60) v1 really matches the user's taste. So v1 is the loud-session EQ by preference, not only because it leaks less bass and can go louder.
- This fits the equal-loudness contours: the louder it gets, the less bass rise is needed ([levels explainer, section 4](../explainer/levels-explained.html)).
