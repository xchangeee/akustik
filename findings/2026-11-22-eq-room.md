# EQ v1 and v2 in the room (2026-11-22)

Data: [`measurements/2026-11-22/`](../measurements/2026-11-22/) sets `eq-seat` (seat + 6 positions), `eq-dj` (7), `eq-corners` (2). At each mic spot v1 (P02, tag `eq1`) and v2 (P03, tag `eq2`) were measured back to back without moving the mic. Compared with the 2026-11-18 flat sets at the same position names.
Script: [analysis/analyse_eq_room.py](../analysis/analyse_eq_room.py). Output: [eq_room.txt](../analysis/output/eq_room.txt), plots [v1](../analysis/output/eq_room_v1.png), [v2](../analysis/output/eq_room_v2.png), [v2 − v1](../analysis/output/eq_room_v2_minus_v1.png), [waterfall](../analysis/output/eq_room_waterfall.png).
"Simulated" = the 2026-11-18 flat IR × the filter files (HPF + PEQ, complex), as in [design_eq.py](../analysis/design_eq.py). All levels are relative to each curve's own 300 Hz–3 kHz level.

## Summary

- **Both EQs do in the room what the simulation predicted.** Measured vs simulated, 25–250 Hz, 1/24 oct: 1.5 dB rms at the seat (L+R), 1.6 at the DJ spot, 1.1–1.2 in the corners. That is the size of the difference from mic placement alone.
- **The DSP filters act in the room exactly as designed.** v2 − v1 at the same mic spot matches the filter files' difference to 0.25–0.8 dB rms (28–300 Hz) at every seat and DJ position.
- **Bass level as designed**, seat L+R, mean 30–150 Hz rel. to mids: flat +6.9 dB, **v1 −0.1** (sim +0.2), **v2 +3.0** (sim +3.3; Harman target +4.5). v2 stays ~3 dB under its target at 30–60 Hz, as designed: a cut-only EQ can't fill the dips.
- **The Sub8 did not play along** on the v1 L sweeps despite P02's broken routing (In A also → Out 3): checked at every spot, see the test section.
- **Ringing at the cut modes is much lower**, e.g. seat L 43 Hz energy left after 100 ms: flat −2.6, v1 −16.9, v2 −11.1 dB. Seat R 84 Hz: −11.0 / −34.3 / −24.4 dB (all re flat at t=0).
- **A narrow resonance at ~78 Hz in the L speaker** (80.0 Hz on 11-18, 77.9 Hz now; T60 ≈ 2.5 s). It was there on 11-18 too, at every L position including the close-ups, so it's not new and not caused by the EQ. Not audible with a 78 Hz sine (user). Section below.

## Mic placement vs 2026-11-18

From the arrival delay of the direct sound (cross-correlation, first arrival, 1 kHz high-pass; section 1 of the output).

- Main seat: within **1.4 cm** of 11-18 (L −0.8, R +1.4 cm), so the loopback-delay placement worked.
- Around the seat and at the DJ spot the positions differ from 11-18 by up to ~14 cm (both days placed by hand). Corners: the direct sound is too weak there to compare (correlation 0.2–0.4).
- eq1 vs eq2 at the same spot: 0 samples apart (once 1 sample). The mic stayed put between the presets.
- Mid level (300 Hz–3 kHz) at the seat and DJ spot is within 0.1 dB of 11-18, so the gain chain is unchanged.

## Narrow resonance at ~78 Hz in the L speaker

Tracked across every IR of both days: [check_nosub.py](../analysis/check_nosub.py) section 6 ([nosub.txt](../analysis/output/nosub.txt)): peak of the late spectrum (0.3–1.0 s) at 70–90 Hz.

- **2026-11-18: at 80.0 Hz (79.7–80.1) in every L IR**: seat, 6 positions, 7 DJ, 2 corners; prominence +12…+21 dB over the 60–100 Hz median. **2026-11-22: at 77.9 Hz (77.7–78.4) in every L IR**, with v1, v2 and without the sub; prominence +13…+24 dB.
- **It sits in or at the L speaker:** in the 11-18 close-ups (mic ~1 cm from the woofer / port, where the room is far below the speaker) the late line is only 14–15 dB below the early 60–100 Hz level, at 79.4 / 80.1 Hz. R's close-ups: 22–23 dB below, at 74–78 Hz. In the room, R shows the line weaker and less consistently (often +5…+9 dB, at varying frequencies).
- Decay at the main seat ≈ T60 2.5 s (Q ≈ 90), far narrower than the room modes (Q 3–8): mechanical, not the room.
- Not in the electrical chain: the DSP electrical measurements (v1 11-18, v2 11-22) have no such line. Amp and speaker weren't part of that check.
- Not the Sub8 (test below). Not the mic stand: the close-ups had the mic at the speaker, and the line is just as clear in the corners (L +12…+21 dB on both days), where the stand lay on the floor (user; standing for all other sets).
- **Correction:** the first version of this file called it new since 11-18, "+10…+23 dB above 11-18 at 76.9 Hz" (eq_room section 5). That compared a fixed narrow band while the line had moved by ~2 Hz. The line itself is about as prominent as on 11-18. Its frequency moved 80.0 → 77.9 Hz (−2.6 %), which suggests something mechanical changed slightly (mounting, feet, a loose part, temperature).
- User: nothing at the L speaker was knowingly touched or moved since 11-18. The cause of the shift stays unknown.
- User: with a 77.8 Hz sine through L (P02, amp 50) nothing audible rings in the room.
- DJ L, weaker: 61.9 Hz, +6…+11 dB at 7 of 7 DJ positions (eq_room section 5), not tracked further.
- Relevance: low. It's too narrow to show in the frequency response, and it isn't audible even with a pure tone. If it's to be found: close-up sweeps at the L woofer, port and cabinet sides, or a hand on the cabinet / base plate during a close-up sweep.

## Sub-out-of-the-room test and the P02 routing

Data: [`measurements/2026-11-22/nosub-seat/`](../measurements/2026-11-22/nosub-seat/), one sweep `1 L seat eq1 nosub` (P02, mic at the main seat, Sub8 out of the room). Script: [analysis/check_nosub.py](../analysis/check_nosub.py), output [nosub.txt](../analysis/output/nosub.txt), [plot](../analysis/output/nosub.png).

- Mic at the same spot as `1 L seat eq1`: direct sound at 443 samples in both (11-18: 444).
- **The 77.8 Hz line is still there without the sub:** late energy (0.3–1.0 s vs 0–0.3 s, 1/24 oct) −1.7 dB without the sub, −0.2 with the sub at C (11-18 at 77.8 Hz: −18.0). Late-spectrum peak 77.9 Hz. So the sub is not the resonator.
- User, after the test: P02's routing was broken, **In A also routed to Out 3**, so the sub might have played along on the v1 L sweeps. **The data say it didn't:**
  - Main seat: `L seat eq1` (sub at C) vs nosub, 40–300 Hz: mean +0.0…+0.2, rms 0.7–0.95 dB. The difference IR is 20–25 dB below the speaker's own, with no arrival from spot C.
  - Every `eq-*` spot: residual eq1 − eq2 × (H_v1 / H_v2), low-passed 200 Hz, is the same for L as for the control R (In B never went to Out 3). Medians seat −25.6 / −25.7 dB, corners −35 / −30, DJ −28 / −27 (L / R). A sub playing the L signal at a usable level would leave a residual near −10 dB or more.
  - So the `eq-*` results above stand. Why the sub stayed silent despite the routing is open (routing changed after the sets? Out 3 muted or at a low fader? sub in standby?). User, 2026-11-22: P02 routing fixed; P03 checked, routed correctly.
- The weaker L fit in the v2 − v1 check (DJ L up to 1.8 dB rms, corners 1.3) sits mostly at 28–35 Hz, in the noise below the HPF (largest single deviation: DJ L back30, 10 dB at 29.6 Hz). It isn't the sub.
