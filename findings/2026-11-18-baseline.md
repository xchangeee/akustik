## DJ position + L+R sum

Data: [`measurements/2026-11-18/dj/`](../measurements/2026-11-18/dj/), 7 spots (pos, left30, right30, fwd30, back30, up30, down30), standing ear height, mic up, 90° file. Positions ball-parked.
L+R = coherent sum of the L and R IRs per position (same mic spot, same loopback timing ref): what is heard when both play the same bass (mono bass, normal for music).
Script: [analysis/analyse_dj_sum.py](../analysis/analyse_dj_sum.py). Plot: [DJ vs seat](../analysis/output/dj_vs_seat.png).
The L direct-sound peak at the DJ spot is weak (the spot is beside L, off-axis), so peak timing jumps there; irrelevant for bass.

Values = L+R position average, relative to the same set's L+R mid level (300 Hz–3 kHz), 1/6 oct:

| Hz | 30 | 43 | 47 | 51 | 55 | 60 | 68 | 80 | 89 | 100 | 115 | 128 | 163 | 210 | 300 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seat | +1.2 | +13.3 | +5.7 | +9.9 | +9.7 | +6.7 | +5.6 | +11.1 | +10.6 | +4.0 | +8.7 | +7.8 | −4.0 | −3.5 | −3.3 |
| DJ | −7.6 | +15.1 | +12.5 | +11.0 | +2.6 | −1.0 | +2.3 | +7.1 | +9.8 | +5.7 | +7.7 | +9.0 | +2.2 | +4.4 | +4.2 |

Findings:
- With both speakers playing, the bass at the seat sits +5…+15 dB above the mids from 40 to 130 Hz: the "boom" in numbers.
- **The DJ spot doesn't lack bass in general.** 43, 51, 89 and 115–128 Hz are as strong as at the seat or stronger (43 Hz +15). The predicted 88 Hz null at the DJ spot doesn't show (89 Hz +9.8).
- The DJ spot does have holes at **30–35 Hz and 55–75 Hz**. At 55–75 Hz, L and R alone are each +5…+9 there, but the sum is ~0: **the two speakers cancel each other at the DJ spot.** EQ can't fix that.
- DJ is +2…+8 dB fuller than the seat from 140 to 300 Hz (near the wall/cupboard).
- EQ consequences: peaks common to seat, DJ and corners: 43 Hz (L and R), 51 Hz (R), 85–89 Hz (R), 115–128 Hz → cut these. Keep any 55–75 Hz cut (R 68 Hz) moderate, or the DJ spot gets thinner there.

## Close-ups woofer/port

Data: [`measurements/2026-11-18/closeups/`](../measurements/2026-11-18/closeups/), mic ~1 cm from the woofer dust cap / in the port opening, pointing at it, **0° cal**, **−40 dBFS** (amp and gain unchanged). Cal file set back to 90° afterwards.
Script: [analysis/analyse_closeups.py](../analysis/analyse_closeups.py). Plot: [close-ups](../analysis/output/closeups.png).

- **Port tuning ≈ 35 Hz (both speakers).** The woofer has no sharp notch, just a broad minimum at ~32–38 Hz, ~15 dB below its midband level. It's probably filled in by port sound reaching the woofer mic (the port is on the same baffle). The port output is flat from ~40 to 85 Hz and falls ~12 dB/oct below ~35 Hz. (Script's automatic notch value of 20 Hz is the search-range edge, ignore.)
- Woofer rolls off from ~90 Hz downward; combined with the port, usable output down to ~35 Hz. Below that the cone is unloaded: excursion rises fast → high-pass needed for protection (DJ material).
- **L and R woofers are identical** within ±1 dB from 30 Hz to 2 kHz, except +3.4 dB on R at 50 Hz (R's strong 51 Hz room mode leaking into the close-up). → **The right speaker itself is fine; R's extra bass is entirely room/position.** (Closes the open "R 2.3 dB louder" question from 2026-11-17.)
- Port pipe resonance visible at ~900 Hz (~7 dB below the woofer close-up level), i.e. port length ≈ 19 cm. Minor.
- High-pass options to simulate in the EQ design: ~30 Hz Butterworth 24 dB/oct (protection, R's 28 Hz room peak −4 dB) or ~35 Hz (stricter, for leakage/DJ preset).
